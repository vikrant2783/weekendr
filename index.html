How to Plan a Weekend Trip with wkndo

  1. Go to wkndo.com — free, no account needed
  2. Select your city from the home screen
  3. Choose Escape and Getaway to drive somewhere new, Explore My City for local discoveries, or Surprise Me for a curated hidden gem
  4. Set your drive time (1 to 5 hours), budget, group size, and vibe
  5. Instantly see hotels with real prices sorted cheapest first, unique stays like treehouses cabins and beachfront homes, bookable Viator activities with exact pricing, and local events
